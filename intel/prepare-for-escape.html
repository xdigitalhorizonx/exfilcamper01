EPISODE:001

Prepare for Escape

The remix that started exfilcamper01

Every raid in Tarkov opens with the same intense anxiety, and every one of us is addicted to it. The loading screen hangs a second too long. Your kit is worth more than your rent. You spawn in, the map is dead quiet, and you already know somebody else loaded in with you.

Every footstep could be theirs. Every door could be the last one you open. Your heart rate doesn't come back down until the extract timer is running, and the second you make it out, you queue up again.

This track lives in that moment.

prepare for escape / hermedic retake
Geneburn · exfilcamper01 remix · released 28 Nov 2023
▶ PLAY IT HERE

What it is

It's my remix of Geneburn's prepare for escape (hermedic retake). It's also the first upload as exfilcamper01. On SoundCloud it's tagged riddim, dark, melodic dubstep, which sums it up: it's heavy, but there's a melody underneath that makes the heavy parts land harder.

How to listen

Headphones, loud, start to finish. The build is what makes the drop land.

Tell me what you think

If this was playing when you got a clutch extract, or when you got head-eyes'd at the last second, I want to hear it. Come tell me in the Emercamp Alliance Discord, or email brandon@digitalhorizon.dev.
