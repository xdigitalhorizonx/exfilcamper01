Prepare for Escape

Every raid in Escape from Tarkov opens with the same four words on the loading screen. You read them so many times they stop meaning anything, until the day they do. Your kit is worth more than your rent, the map is quiet, and you know somebody else loaded in with you.

This track lives in that moment.

prepare for escape / hermedic retake
Geneburn · exfilcamper01 remix · released 28 Nov 2023
▶ LOAD INTO RAID

What it is

It's my remix of Geneburn's prepare for escape (hermedic retake). It's also the first upload under the exfilcamper01 callsign. On SoundCloud it's tagged riddim, dark, melodic dubstep, which sums it up: it's heavy, but there's a melody underneath that makes the heavy parts land harder.

How to listen

Headphones, loud. On this site the red IMPACT markers on the waveform show where the energy jumps hardest. Hit D to skip straight to the next one. But run it start to finish at least once. The build is the raid; the drop is the extract.

Survive the full track and the site counts it as a raid survived. It lives in your browser; nobody else sees it.
