    Deathcore breakdowns, run through a subwoofer.

    exfilcamper01 makes dark, sinister riddim and darkstep. Originally a jazz drummer and pianist, he progressed into heavier and darker realms as his perception of the world changed. Through many years as a professional death metal drummer, touring with a lot of well-known bands, and an eventual mild obsession with OG dubstep artists like Flux Pavilion, Feed Me, Skrillex and Nero… the music you hear today was born. It's dubstep written like deathcore rhythmically, but with melodic storytelling depicting the ups and downs you often experience living life.

    Raised in Las Vegas, NV. Computer-obsessed since age 6, exfilcamper01 eventually earned his degrees from UNLV and went on to found the business he operates today (with a few, ahem… setbacks, of course; if you know, you know). He continues to spend his days writing visceral bangers, building his company, and camping you geeks just for the rage messages.
