    Dark, sinister dubstep written for the moment the raid goes wrong.

    exfilcamper01 makes riddim and darkstep that come straight off the screen. Hours sunk into Escape from Tarkov: the dead-quiet corridor, footsteps you can't place, an extract timer bleeding out while your gear is worth more than your life. That dread gets pressed into the low end.

    Heavy, cold, and built for headphones. The drops hit like a breach charge and the space between them is left uncomfortably empty on purpose.

    The project also runs as zero, “born of the black wretch.” Two tracks are out in the wild. The rest are still in the stash.
